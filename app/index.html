CheeseTV is a blank player. No channels are included. Add your own playlist to start watching.

Playlists

Your playlist and login are saved only on this device (browser storage). CheeseTV never sends them to our servers.

Why won't some channels play in a browser?

Browsers have security rules that apps don't. A secure page (https) is not allowed to load http:// streams or playlists ("mixed content"), and the provider's server must allow web access (CORS). Many IPTV providers only offer http or block browsers. Those channels still work in the CheeseTV Android / Google TV app, on Roku, and in the upcoming CheeseTV iPhone app.
