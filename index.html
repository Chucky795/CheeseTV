📺 Record from your phone, watch on your TV

Out and about? Open CheeseTV on your phone or in the web player, tap a show in the Guide (or 📺 On my TV while watching) and choose Record on my TV. The CheeseTV app on your TV at home, signed in to the same login, records it, even from standby, and it's waiting in the TV's DVR tab when you get home. Your plan's single connection means one recording at a time; recordings stay on your TV, never on our servers.

Download the App

Get CheeseTV on your device

📱Android PhoneDownload APK
  1. Tap the button above.
  2. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the file from your notifications.
  4. Allow installs from Chrome if asked, then tap Install.
 🔥Fire TV & Android TVDownload APK
  1. On your Fire Stick, install the Downloader app.
  2. Open Downloader and type watchcheesetv.com/tv.apk
  3. Tap Install when it finishes.
 📺RokuDownload Roku ZIP
  1. Download the zip on a computer.
  2. On the Roku remote press Home ×3, Up ×2, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right.
  3. Turn on Developer Mode and note the IP shown.
  4. Open that IP in a browser and upload the zip.
 🍏iPhone & WebOpen Web Player
  1. Tap the button above.
  2. Or type http://live.watchcheesetv.com in Safari or Chrome.
  3. Sign in and start watching.

iPhone / Web: watch in your browser

Open the CheeseTV Web Player on iPhone, iPad or computer and add your playlist.

Add to Home Screen on iPhone: open watchcheesetv.com/app in Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add. CheeseTV opens full screen like an app. Some channels only play in the Android or Roku app because of browser security rules.
