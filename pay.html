Questions

Is Zelle safe?

Zelle is run by the big US banks and moves money directly between bank accounts. Be aware that Zelle payments work like cash: once sent they usually can't be reversed and there is no buyer protection. That's why we offer a free trial first, and why you should always double-check you're sending to cheesetv795@gmail.com.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes. Start a free 12-hour trial and only pay once you're happy.

Do you take cards or other apps?

We only take Zelle. We'll never ask for your card number.

My bank doesn't have Zelle

You can download the Zelle app and link a US debit card or bank account there.
