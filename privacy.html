CheeseTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy covers the CheeseTV website (watchcheesetv.com) and the CheeseTV Android app, which shows that website.

What we collect

We don't require an account and we don't collect personal information automatically. If you use the contact form, we get the name, email address and message you enter, and we use them only to reply to you. Form messages are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).

Third-party services

TV listings are loaded from TVmaze (tvmaze.com), and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, these services may log basic technical details such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers. The app may store website files on your device so pages load faster and work offline.

Children

CheeseTV isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your information.

Contact

For questions, email support@watchcheesetv.com.
